Slide 2: Problem Statement Slide 02 / 07

The Busy Chaiwala & Udhaar Crisis

Over 20 million street vendors (chaiwalas, kirana stores, food stalls) operate in high-rush, noisy environments where conventional digital apps completely fail.

💧 Wet Hands, Boiling Tea

Touchscreens are impossible to use while milk is boiling, dishes are washing, or snacks are frying. Vendors cannot stop during a rush to type.

🧠 Rapid Mental Math Errors

When 10 customers shout orders at once, vendors lose track of who ordered what and make calculation mistakes, causing chronic daily revenue leaks.

📓 ₹5,000+ Monthly Lost Udhaar

Chalkboards and paper notebooks get erased, torn, or forgotten. Uncollected credit accounts for huge financial loss every single month.

Target: 20M+ Street Vendors in India Addresses Real Everyday Economic Friction
Slide 3: Proposed Solution Slide 03 / 07

Bol-Khata: The Conversational Voice POS

A 1-tap voice assistant built for the "10-Second Usability Rule". The vendor taps the mic (or Bluetooth earphone) and speaks naturally.

🗣️ 1. Natural Spoken Input

Vendor speaks in Hindi/Hinglish:
"Ramesh bhai 2 chai aur bun maska udhaar" or "सुरेश का 40 रुपये काटो".

⚙️ 2. Automated AI Ledger

AI extracts customer name, auto-computes menu prices, automatically creates new accounts, or deducts payments.

🔊 3. Voice Feedback + UPI

Loud Soundbox feedback confirms entry: "रमेश के खाते में ₹35 जोड़े गए" + generates 1-click WhatsApp UPI link.

Zero Typing • Zero Forms • 100% Hands-Free Bridges Khatabook + Paytm Soundbox
Slide 4: Technology Stack Slide 04 / 07

Modern, Lightweight & Low-Cost Stack

🎨 Frontend & Voice Engine

  • • HTML5 / Tailwind CSS: High-contrast, mobile-first responsive UI.
  • • Web Speech API: Zero-latency, on-device native speech recognition in Hindi (`hi-IN`) and English.
  • • SpeechSynthesis API: Native Hindi/Indian voice feedback (Soundbox experience).

🧠 AI Parser & Database Architecture

  • • Gemini 1.5 Flash / Smart Parser: Sub-second extraction of customer entity, items, and credit/payment intent.
  • • LocalStorage & SQLite (ACID): Persistent ledger database with transaction audit logs.
  • • NPCI UPI Deep Linking: Standard `upi://pay` generation for instant WhatsApp settlement.
Runs on any low-cost smartphone Low-bandwidth friendly (2G/3G ready)
Slide 5: Implementation & Features Slide 05 / 07

Key Working Features

✨ Auto Account Creation & Cutting

• Auto-Creates Accounts: Just say a new customer name (*"अमित 50 रुपये उधार"*), and their ledger card is instantly created.
• Automatic Deduction: Saying *"रमेश का 30 रुपये काटो"* or *"सुरेश ने 40 दिए"* instantly cuts/clears the balance.

📲 WhatsApp Reminders & Audit Log

• 1-Click WhatsApp UPI: Sends polite message with direct UPI payment link to clear pending credit.
• Voice Audit Trail: Stores timestamped spoken transcripts to resolve disputes if a customer questions a bill.
