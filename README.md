# Synaptic_duo_VibeXcodes_Hackathon
Bol-Khata is a zero-typing voice AI ledger designed for street vendors and chaiwalas. By speaking naturally in Hindi or Hinglish, vendors can auto-create customer cards, log udhaar, deduct payments, and send WhatsApp UPI links with real-time Soundbox audio confirmation—recovering ₹5,000+ in forgotten credit every month.




#Key Features of project
Unlike basic chatbots, Bol-Khata is a fully working, real-time ledger with two standout features:

First, Automatic Account Creation: If a vendor says a brand new customer name — like 'अमित 50 रुपये उधार' — a dedicated customer card is instantly created with zero manual setup.

Second, Smart Amount Deduction: When a customer pays, saying 'रमेश का 30 रुपये काटो' or 'सुरेश ने 40 जमा किए' automatically cuts the amount from their balance and announces the remaining debt.

It also generates pre-filled WhatsApp UPI links and maintains an immutable voice audit trail so shopkeepers and customers never argue over forgotten bills.




#Project Description
If you look at a local chaiwala during morning rush hours, his hands are constantly wet with boiling milk, tea glasses, and washing water. When 10 customers shout orders at the same time, he cannot unlock a smartphone, open an app, and type.

Because of this, vendors rely on scraps of paper or chalkboards. They make mental calculation mistakes, forget who owes what, and lose ₹3,000 to ₹7,000 every single month in uncollected udhaar. This is a real everyday economic problem for micro-workers."

"To solve this, we built Bol-Khata around the 10-Second Usability Rule.

Instead of typing, the shopkeeper simply taps one giant microphone button — or a cheap Bluetooth earbud — and speaks naturally in Hindi or Hinglish:

For example: 'Ramesh bhai 2 chai aur bun maska udhaar' or 'सुरेश का 40 रुपये काटो'.

Bol-Khata's AI automatically extracts the customer's name, calculates the item prices, updates the ledger, and speaks confirmation back out loud like a smart Soundbox, while preparing an instant 1-click WhatsApp UPI payment link.
